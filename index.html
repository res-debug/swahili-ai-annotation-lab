Annotation guidelines

Read these before labeling. Consistent definitions produce usable training data.

Sentiment

Positive
Clearly favorable emotion, gratitude, or satisfaction.
Negative
Frustration, anger, dissatisfaction, or complaint tone.
Neutral
Factual or informational with no clear emotional polarity.

Intent

Question
Seeks information; often ends with a question mark or interrogative.
Request
Asks for an action, favor, or service politely.
Complaint
Reports a problem or dissatisfaction with a service/experience.
Greeting
Opens a conversation or exchange of pleasantries.
Statement
Declares information without asking or demanding.

Topic

Health
Medical, wellness, clinics, symptoms.
Transport
Matatu, boda boda, traffic, travel.
Money
M-Pesa, prices, payments, savings.
Education
Schools, exams, teachers, learning.
Food
Meals, markets, restaurants, cooking.
Family
Relatives, household, parenting.
Weather
Rain, sun, seasons, climate.
Other
Anything outside the above categories.

Language

Swahili
Predominantly Swahili (with standard loanwords acceptable).
English
Predominantly English text.
Code-switching
Meaningful mixing of Swahili and English in the same sentence.

Workflow rules

  1. Every sentence requires all four labels; incomplete rows are flagged.
  2. Use Skip if genuinely ambiguous; skipped rows remain unlabeled.
  3. Notes are optional but encouraged for edge cases.
  4. Progress is saved automatically to your browser (localStorage).
